Filming tips
  • Tripod, not handheld. Set it at hip height, 10–15 ft away, square to the pitcher's path (camera looking at the pitcher's chest during leg lift).
  • Whole body in frame from leg lift through the finish, with a little room on both sides. Landscape works best.
  • Use slo-mo (120 or 240 fps) if you can. It makes foot plant and release much more precise. If the file you export plays slowed down, set "Clip speed" to match.
  • Good light, plain background. Avoid other people walking through the frame.
  • One pitch per clip. Trim to the delivery before or after upload.

What this version measures

Single camera, 2D. Body points come from Google's MediaPipe pose model, running on your device. Video is not uploaded anywhere. Key frames (peak leg lift, foot plant, release) are found automatically; check them with the buttons under the video. Rotation metrics (kinematic sequence, hip-shoulder separation, flying open) need 3D and are not included yet. Treat numbers as trends for one athlete on a consistent camera setup, not lab-grade values.
